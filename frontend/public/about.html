Discover what's playing at cinemas in your city, keep track of the movies you want to see, and coordinate showtimes with friends.

MiKiNO aggregates showtimes from cinemas near you so you can browse, filter, and find something to watch. Save movies to your watchlist, see when friends are going to the same showing, and get notified about screenings you care about.

You can create a MiKiNO account with your email address, or sign in with your Google or Apple account for faster, one-tap access — we only use this to identify your account and never post or share anything on your behalf.
